How this works

Clean

Strip URLs, HTML and stray whitespace while preserving case and punctuation — VADER reads "GREAT!!" as more intense than "great".

Tokenize

NLTK's word_tokenize splits the input into words and punctuation tokens for downstream analysis.

Filter

Stopwords (the, is, and …) are removed to surface the keywords actually carrying meaning.

Score

VADER's lexicon + rule-based model returns compound, positive, neutral and negative scores, thresholded into a final label.
